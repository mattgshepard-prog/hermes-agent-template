## Recipe Selection Guidelines

**Good fits:**
- Sheet-pan meals (easy customization)
- Chicken-based (safe for all)
- High-protein options (supports Matt's goals)
- Can be served bland with spice on the side
- Generate leftovers
- Lactose-free or easily modified
- Whole foods: fresh meat, vegetables, minimal processing
- Balanced macros: protein, complex carbs, vegetables

**Protein priorities:**
- Chicken breasts/thighs
- Lean ground beef/turkey
- Fish (for Matt - not shared with Amy except crab)
- Eggs
- Greek yogurt (lactose-free for shared meals)
- Beans/legumes as sides

**Vegetable priorities:**
- Broccoli (fresh) - household staple
- Cauliflower (fresh) - household staple
- Baby carrots (snacks, cooking, guinea pig food)
- Frozen vegetable mixes
- Fresh vegetables for balanced meals

**Avoid:**
- Seafood (except crab)
- Heavy dairy in base (unless using lactose-free alternative)
- Very spicy without ability to separate
- Single-serving sizes (need leftovers)
- Heavily processed ingredients
- High-carb, low-protein meals
