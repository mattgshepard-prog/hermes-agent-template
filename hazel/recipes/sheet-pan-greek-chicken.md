# Sheet-Pan Greek Chicken

**Source:** Serious Eats  
**Author:** Leah Colins  
**Updated:** July 21, 2026  
**Prep Time:** 25 mins  
**Cook Time:** 30 mins  
**Marinating Time:** 27 mins  
**Total Time:** 1 hr 22 mins  
**Servings:** 4 to 6

## Description
Two sauces, one pan, and a clever roasting trick turn simple chicken and veggies into a weeknight-easy, dinner-party-worthy meal.

## Ingredients

- 2 tablespoons (30 ml) fresh lemon juice (from 1 medium lemon)
- 1 tablespoon (15 ml) red wine vinegar
- 2 teaspoons chopped fresh thyme
- 1 teaspoon dried oregano
- 1/2 teaspoon black pepper
- 1/2 teaspoon ground coriander
- 8 tablespoons (120 ml) extra-virgin olive oil, divided
- 2 1/2 teaspoons Diamond Crystal kosher salt, divided; for table salt, use half as much by volume
- 4 medium garlic cloves, grated (about 2 teaspoons), divided
- 6 bone-in, skin-on chicken thighs
- 10 ounces English cucumber (283 g; about 1 medium cucumber), peeled, seeded, and grated on large holes of box grater (about 1 cup shredded)
- 1 cup (240 ml) plain whole-milk strained Greek-style yogurt
- 2 tablespoons chopped fresh mint and/or dill, plus more for garnish

## Instructions

### Step 1
In a large bowl or zip-top plastic bag, whisk together lemon juice, vinegar, thyme, oregano, black pepper, coriander, 6 tablespoons (90ml) olive oil, 2 teaspoons salt, and half the grated garlic. Add chicken thighs and toss to coat. Refrigerate for at least 15 minutes or up to 8 hours.

### Step 2
Meanwhile, in a medium bowl, stir together cucumber, yogurt, mint and/or dill, remaining garlic, remaining 2 tablespoons (30ml) olive oil, and remaining 1/2 teaspoon salt. Cover and refrigerate until ready to serve.

### Step 3
Adjust oven rack to middle position. Set an empty 13-by-18-inch rimmed baking sheet on rack and preheat oven to 425°F (220°C).

### Step 4
Using kitchen towels or oven mitts, carefully remove hot sheet pan from oven. Pour 2 tablespoons (30ml) olive oil onto hot sheet pan and return to oven; heat oil until it is hot and shimmering, about 10 minutes.

### Step 5
Using kitchen towels or oven mitts, carefully remove sheet pan from oven. Remove chicken from marinade, shaking off excess, and place skin-side down on hot oiled sheet pan. Bake until skin is browned and crispy, about 15 minutes.

### Step 6
Using tongs, flip chicken skin-side up. Bake until chicken is cooked through and registers 165°F (74°C) on an instant-read thermometer inserted into thickest part of thigh, 10 to 15 minutes longer.

### Step 7
Transfer chicken to a serving platter. Serve with yogurt sauce, garnished with additional mint and/or dill.

## Special Equipment
13- by-18-inch rimmed baking sheet, instant-read thermometer, tongs, box grater

## Notes
For best results, marinate chicken for at least 15 minutes, or up to 8 hours for deeper flavor.

The cucumber yogurt sauce (tzatziki) can be made up to 1 day ahead and refrigerated.

## Make-Ahead and Storage
Chicken can be marinated up to 8 hours in advance. 

Yogurt sauce can be made 1 day ahead and stored covered in the refrigerator.

Leftovers can be refrigerated in an airtight container for up to 3 days. Reheat chicken in oven at 350°F until warmed through. Serve with chilled yogurt sauce.
